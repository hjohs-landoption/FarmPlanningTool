1. Executive Overview

Bottom Line Up Front

Client is positioned to generate significant new revenue by "stacking" federal conservation programs with private carbon markets. Historically resistant to cover crops, the selection of EQIP 340 signals a willingness to test them.

Landowner Profile

Summary

Multi-generational farmer. 4,800 acres Corn/Soy with Hog Barns.

Family Context

Goal: Succession to son/grandson. Legacy: Soil preservation is key priority.

Operational

Min/No-Till on all acres. Hog manure (fall) & NH3.

Financial

ROI-driven. Addresses high inputs. Opportunity: Manure acres.
